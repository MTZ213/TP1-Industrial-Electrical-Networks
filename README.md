# TP N°01 – Industrial Electrical Networks

### Cable Sizing and Protection of Low-Voltage Industrial Installations

---

## 1. Project Overview

This project focuses on the sizing of electrical conductors and the selection of protection devices in a three-phase low-voltage industrial installation.

The study is based on a practical case involving four electrical feeders supplied by a 400 V transformer.

## 2. Technical Specifications

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| Supply voltage | 400 V |
| Installation type | Three-phase |
| Ambient temperature | 40 °C |
| Protection | Circuit breakers |
| Conductor materials | Copper / Aluminium |
| Insulation types | PVC / PR |

## 3. Studied Feeders

| Feeder | Apparent Power | Cable Length |
|---|---:|---:|
| Dep 1 (AD) | 15 kVA | 65 m |
| Dep 2 (CE) | 8.1 kVA | 70 m |
| Dep 3 (MG) | 21 kVA | 70 m |
| Dep 4 (CM) | 29 kVA | 75 m |

## 4. Methodology

The study involves:

- Calculation of operating currents.
- Application of correction factors.
- Determination of cable cross-sections.
- Comparison between copper and aluminium conductors.
- Evaluation of PVC and PR insulation.
- Analysis of temperature and installation methods.
- Voltage drop verification.
